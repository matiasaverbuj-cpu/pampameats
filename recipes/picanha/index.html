Picanha al Asador

Serves 4–6  ·  25 Min  ·  Medium-Rare
Ingredients
  • 1 Pampa Meats Picanha (~2.5 lb)
  • Coarse sea salt
  • Chimichurri, to serve (optional)
Method
  1. Rest at room temperature 30–40 min. Pat dry.
  2. Score the fat cap in a crosshatch, ¼-in deep — not into the meat.
  3. Salt generously on every side with coarse salt.
  4. Sear fat-side down on high heat until golden, 6–8 min.
  5. Turn and cook to medium-rare, 130°F / 54°C internal.
  6. Rest 10 min. Slice against the grain; serve with chimichurri.

Whole Roasted Picanha

Serves 4–6  ·  45 Min  ·  Oven
Ingredients
  • 1 Pampa Meats Picanha (~2.5 lb)
  • Coarse sea salt & cracked black pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic, halved
  • Fresh rosemary or thyme (optional)
Method
  1. Heat oven to 425°F / 220°C. Rest the meat 30 min at room temperature.
  2. Score the fat cap, rub all over with garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Sear fat-side down in a hot, oven-safe pan until deeply golden, 5 min.
  4. Transfer fat-side up to the oven. Roast 20–25 min to 130°F / 54°C for medium-rare.
  5. Rest 12–15 min, tented loosely.
  6. Slice against the grain into thick steaks.

Picanha Skewers

Serves 4–6  ·  20 Min  ·  Grill
Ingredients
  • 1 Pampa Meats Picanha (~2.5 lb)
  • Coarse sea salt
  • Long metal skewers
Method
  1. Cut the picanha with the grain into 1½-in thick slices, fat cap on.
  2. Bend each slice into a C and thread onto skewers, fat facing out.
  3. Salt generously with coarse salt.
  4. Grill over high heat, turning, until the fat crisps and the edges char, 10–12 min.
  5. Rest 5 min off the heat.
  6. Slice the seared outer layer off each skewer; return the rest to the grill and repeat.
