Roasted Tenderloin / Filet Mignon

Serves 6–8  ·  35 Min  ·  Oven
Ingredients
  • 1 Pampa Meats Tenderloin (~2.5 lb)
  • Coarse salt & cracked pepper
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic & thyme
Method
  1. Heat oven to 425°F / 220°C. Tie the tenderloin for even cooking.
  2. Rub with oil, garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Sear all sides in a hot pan until browned, 4–5 min total.
  4. Roast to 125°F / 52°C for medium-rare, 18–22 min.
  5. Rest 12 min, tented loosely.
  6. Slice into thick medallions.

Pan-Seared Filet

Serves 2  ·  15 Min  ·  Cast Iron
Ingredients
  • 2 tenderloin steaks (filet), 1.5-in thick
  • Coarse salt
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic & thyme (optional)
Method
  1. Rest at room temperature 30 min. Pat dry; salt well.
  2. Heat cast iron until smoking; add a film of oil.
  3. Sear 3 min per side without moving, building a crust.
  4. Sear the edges, basting with the hot oil, garlic and thyme.
  5. Pull at 130°F / 54°C. Rest 5 min.

Grilled Tenderloin Medallions

Serves 4  ·  12 Min  ·  Grill
Ingredients
  • Tenderloin cut into 1.5-in medallions
  • Coarse sea salt
  • Olive oil
  • Chimichurri, to serve
Method
  1. Oil and salt the medallions; rest 20 min.
  2. Grill over high heat 3 min per side for clean marks.
  3. Move to a cooler zone if needed to reach temperature gently.
  4. Pull at 130°F / 54°C for medium-rare.
  5. Rest 5 min; serve with chimichurri.
