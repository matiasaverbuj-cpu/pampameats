Reverse-Seared New York Strip

Serves 2  ·  35 Min  ·  Cast Iron
Ingredients
  • 2 New York Strip steaks, 1.5-in thick
  • Coarse salt & cracked pepper
  • Olive oil
Method
  1. Heat oven to 250°F / 120°C. Season generously and set on a rack.
  2. Roast until the center reaches 115°F / 46°C, 20–25 min.
  3. Rest while a cast-iron pan heats until smoking.
  4. Sear in oil 60–90 sec per side, plus the fat edge, until deeply crusted.
  5. Pull at 130°F / 54°C. Rest 5 min before slicing.

Grilled New York Strip

Serves 2  ·  15 Min  ·  Grill
Ingredients
  • 2 New York Strip steaks, 1.25-in thick
  • Coarse sea salt
  • Olive oil
Method
  1. Rest at room temperature 30 min. Pat dry, oil lightly, salt well.
  2. Grill over high heat 3–4 min until well-marked.
  3. Flip, render the fat edge against the grates, cook 3–4 min more.
  4. Pull at 130°F / 54°C for medium-rare.
  5. Rest 5 min; slice against the grain.

Smoked New York Strip

Serves 2–4  ·  1.5 Hr  ·  Smoker
Ingredients
  • New York Strip steaks, 1.5-in thick
  • Coarse salt & pepper
  • Wood (oak or hickory)
Method
  1. Set the smoker to 225°F / 107°C. Season the steaks.
  2. Smoke until the center reaches 115°F / 46°C, about 60–75 min.
  3. Rest 10 min while you heat a grill or pan screaming hot.
  4. Sear hard 60 sec per side for a crust.
  5. Pull at 130°F / 54°C. Rest and slice.
