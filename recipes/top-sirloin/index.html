Reverse-Seared Top Sirloin

Serves 4  ·  40 Min  ·  Reverse Sear
Ingredients
  • 1 Pampa Meats Top Sirloin (~5 lb)
  • Coarse sea salt & cracked pepper
  • Olive oil
Method
  1. Heat oven (or grill, indirect) to 250°F / 120°C. Season the roast all over.
  2. Cook slowly until the center reaches 115°F / 46°C, about 30 min.
  3. Rest 10 min while you heat a pan or grill as hot as possible.
  4. Sear all sides in olive oil until a deep crust forms, 1–2 min per side.
  5. Pull at 130°F / 54°C for medium-rare. Rest 8 min.
  6. Slice thin against the grain.

Cast-Iron Top Sirloin

Serves 4  ·  20 Min  ·  Stovetop
Ingredients
  • Top Sirloin steaks, 1-in thick
  • Coarse salt
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic & rosemary (optional)
Method
  1. Rest steaks at room temperature 30 min. Pat very dry; salt well.
  2. Heat cast iron until smoking. Add a film of oil.
  3. Sear undisturbed 3 min until a crust forms; flip and sear 2–3 min more.
  4. Add garlic and rosemary in the last minute, spooning the oil over the meat.
  5. Pull at 130°F / 54°C. Rest 5 min; slice against the grain.

Cuadril al Asador

Serves 4–6  ·  30 Min  ·  Grill
Ingredients
  • 1 Pampa Meats Top Sirloin (~5 lb)
  • Coarse sea salt
  • Chimichurri, to serve
Method
  1. Build a hot side and a cooler side on the grill.
  2. Salt generously. Start over the cooler side, lid down, turning every 8 min.
  3. When the center hits 120°F / 49°C, move to the hot side.
  4. Sear hard on every face until charred and crusted, 6–8 min total.
  5. Pull at 130°F / 54°C. Rest 10 min.
  6. Slice against the grain; serve with chimichurri.
