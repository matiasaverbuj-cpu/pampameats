How to add an order

Super simple — about 1 minute. You've got this. 👍

1
Open the website

Go to pampameats.com/dashboard and type the password.

2
Tap the gold "A/R" box

It's the big box that says A/R — Sales & Receivables.

A/R
3
Tap "New customer order"

The first button at the top.

New customer order
4
Tap your name

Under "Taken by", tap your name (so we know who took the order).

Taken by → your name
5
Type the customer's name and phone

Just the name and the phone number. That's all.

6
Pick the cuts with + and −

For each cut (Picanha, Top Sirloin, New York Strip, Tenderloin), tap + to add packages or − to remove.

＋ / −
7
Tap "Save"

The order is in! It shows up for everyone automatically.

Save
✅

That's it!

The order is saved. No paper, no WhatsApp to remember.

Golden rule: add the order the moment the customer asks. One minute now saves a lot later — and nothing gets lost.
