How to add an order

Takes about a minute. Works right from your phone — no paper, nothing to forget.

~1 minute Phone friendly Syncs for everyone
7 simple steps
1

Open the dashboard

Go to pampameats.com/dashboard and enter the password.

2

Open A/R

Tap the gold box that says A/R — Sales & Receivables.

A/R
3

New customer order

Tap the New customer order button at the top.

New customer order
4

Who's taking it

Under Taken by, tap your name — so we know who took the order.

Taken by → your name
5

Customer details

Type the customer's name and phone. That's all you need.

6

Choose the cuts

Use + and − for each cut — Picanha, Top Sirloin, New York Strip, Tenderloin.

＋ / −
7

Save

Tap Save. The order is in — and shows up for everyone instantly.

Save

That's it — order saved

It appears on the dashboard and in A/R right away. No paper, no WhatsApp to remember.

Two ways an order gets in
Option A · You add it

Add it yourself

Follow the 7 steps above whenever a customer asks you directly. Best for phone calls and in-person orders.

Option B · Customer adds it

Let the customer order

Share the store link — the customer picks their cuts and reserves. The order lands in the system automatically.

pampameats.com/order →
Golden rule: add the order the moment the customer asks. One minute now saves a lot later — and nothing gets lost.
