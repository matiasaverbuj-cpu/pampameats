RABBINICAL SUPERVISION

Chacham Yitzchak R. Nehmad

Shlit"a · Buenos Aires
◆

Born in Buenos Aires in 1952, Rabbi Nehmad descends from a distinguished line of Shojatim from Aram Tzoba (Halab) — his grandfather, Chacham Yitzchak Nehmad, was among the first Shojatim of Buenos Aires.

He studied for four years at the renowned Yeshivat Porat Yosef in Jerusalem under Chacham Shalom Cohen zt"l, receiving Semijá as Shojet and Bodek from Rabbi Nissim Azran.

For nearly 50 years he has led Shejitá operations in Argentina, including the export program to the United States under Neve Tzión, the certification body directed by Chacham Shalom Cohen zt"l.

Today, his team personally supervises every cut that leaves our facility.

THE ART OF NIQUR

Reviving a biblical tradition

◆

For thousands of years, the hindquarter was reserved only for those willing to master Niqur — the meticulous removal of the sciatic nerve and forbidden fats according to Halajá.

Few do it today. Almost none for export. We do — so the kosher table doesn't have to settle for less.
