The customer store

pampameats.com/order — the public link you send to customers
pampameats.com/order

Reserve my order
  1. Send the link pampameats.com/order to a customer (text / WhatsApp / social).
  2. They tap + / − on each cut — the total updates live.
  3. They choose NY or NJ, a delivery date, type name / phone / address.
  4. They hit Reserve — the order lands in your dashboard automatically.
ExampleA customer taps "+" twice on Picanha, picks NJ + Friday, and reserves. You didn't lift a finger — the order is already in your Sales screen.

Charge the exact weight

"Weigh & Invoice" — meat is sold by weight, so the final price depends on the real pounds
/dashboard/weigh
WEIGH & INVOICE · David Cohen
Picanha — actual weight12.4 lb
12.4 lb × $12/lb$148.80
Save final invoice
  1. Open Weigh & Invoice, pick the order.
  2. Type the real weight per cut from the scale.
  3. The system multiplies by the price/lb and shows the exact total.
  4. Save — that becomes the customer's final invoice.
ExampleThe scale shows 12.4 lb of Picanha. Type 12.4 → it shows $148.80 to charge. Exact, every time.

Purchasing — orders & invoices

The "A/P" screen does two things: send Purchase Orders to the supplier, and record the invoices you owe
A. Create a Purchase Order (what you're buying)
/dashboard/ap → Create PO
CREATE PURCHASE ORDER
Supplier: Our supplier ▾
NY Strip3 cases
Picanha2 cases
Send PO
  1. Dashboard → A/P → Create PO.
  2. Choose the supplier and enter how many cases/units per cut (the Picking list tells you the amounts).
  3. Hit Send PO — it emails the order to the supplier from purchasing@pampameats.com.
ExampleThe Picking list says you need 3 cases NY Strip + 2 Picanha. Create the PO with those amounts, hit Send — the supplier gets a clean order from your Pampa purchasing address.
B. Log the supplier invoice (what you owe)
/dashboard/ap
LOAD A VENDOR INVOICE
Supplier: Our supplier ▾
Invoice # 88245 · Amount $2,140.00
Invoice date 06/27/2026 · Due 07/27/2026
Add bill
  1. On the A/P screen, choose the supplier and type invoice # + amount + dates (MM/DD/YYYY).
  2. Attach the PDF and add line items if you want → Add bill.
  3. When you pay it, hit Mark paid.
ExampleAn invoice arrives for $2,140. Load it, attach the PDF, save. Now the system knows exactly what you owe and when it's due.
