OUR STORY

From the open Pampas
to the kosher table.

Where Argentina’s grasslands have produced the world’s most celebrated beef for generations — now, for the first time, brought to the kosher tables of New York & New Jersey.

◆

The land

The Pampas are the vast, open grasslands at the heart of Argentina — the reason Argentine beef is spoken of in the same breath as the finest in the world. Our Angus cattle are grass-fed on open pasture: no feedlots, no shortcuts. It is a slower, older way of raising beef, and you taste the difference in every cut.

The cuts

We focus on the hindquarter — the crown cuts of the Argentine asado: Picanha, Top Sirloin, New York Strip and Tenderloin / Filet Mignon. Steakhouse quality, hand-trimmed by trained experts. Not sure where to start? Our cut finder matches you with the right one in three questions.

Under rabbinical supervision

Every cut is Glatt Kosher, Beit Yosef — the highest Sephardic kashrut standard, accepted by all communities. Our Shechita is led by Rabbi Yitzchak R. Nehmad (Shlita) of Buenos Aires, who descends from a distinguished line of Shochatim and has supervised kosher operations in Argentina — including the export program to the United States — for nearly fifty years. His team personally oversees every cut that leaves our facility.

How it reaches you

Beef is flash-frozen at the source and delivered frozen, cold-chain, across New York & New Jersey — never thawed in transit, so it arrives exactly as intended. You order online, we send a quote (usually within ~30 minutes), and we schedule your delivery.
