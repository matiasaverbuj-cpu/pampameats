How Pampa Meats works

Everything in one place — in about 2 minutes.

1

Log in

Go to pampameats.com/dashboard and enter the password Mati shares with you. You'll land on a simple menu with two modules: A/R and A/P.

2

A/R — Sales & Receivables

Take a new customer order, generate an invoice, and see who owes us and who already paid. This is your sales side.

3

A/P — Purchasing & Payables

Create a PO to our supplier (RB), load the vendor invoice (PDF + amount), and track what we owe. This is your buying side.

4

Daily Brief

Every morning you get an email with the numbers: new orders, revenue, what's collected, what's outstanding, and profit. No need to log in to stay on top.

5

Sales & Gross Profit

Under Finance & Setup you'll find the sales overview (charts) and gross profit — revenue minus cost — at a glance.
