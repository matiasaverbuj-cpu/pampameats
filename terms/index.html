Terms & Refund Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Pampa Meats is the retail brand of AliKosher Inc (Pembroke Pines, Florida). We sell premium Argentine Angus beef, Glatt Kosher and Beit Yosef certified, delivered frozen across New York and New Jersey. By placing an order you agree to the terms below.

1. Products & Kosher Certification

All beef is Glatt Kosher, Beit Yosef certified, and rabbinically supervised. We specialize in hindquarter cuts, fully deveined (niqur). Product images are illustrative; natural cuts vary in size, color, and marbling.

2. Pricing & Catch-Weight

Our beef is sold by the pound (catch-weight). The total shown when you place an order is an estimate based on average cut weights. Because every cut is weighed individually, your final price is calculated from the exact weight of the cuts prepared for your order. We confirm the final weight and total with you before payment is due.

3. Ordering & Payment

  • Placing an order online is a reservation, not a final charge.
  • After your cuts are weighed, we send you a final invoice for the exact amount.
  • We accept payment by credit/debit card (processed securely by Stripe) and by Zelle. Card statements will show PAMPA MEATS.
  • Your order is confirmed once payment of the final invoice is received.

4. Shipping & Delivery

We deliver frozen, cold-chain, within New York and New Jersey. Delivery windows are arranged directly with you. Please provide an accurate address and ensure someone is available to receive a perishable, temperature-sensitive order. Risk of loss passes to you upon delivery.

5. Refund & Return Policy

Your satisfaction matters to us. Because our products are perishable food, we cannot accept returns of delivered product. However:

  • Please inspect your order at the time of delivery.
  • If any item arrives damaged, incorrect, or not up to our quality standard, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo.
  • For verified issues we will, at our discretion, provide a replacement, store credit, or refund of the affected item to your original payment method.
  • Refunds to card are processed through Stripe and typically appear within 5–10 business days.

6. Cancellations & Changes

Orders can be changed or cancelled at no charge up until your cuts have been weighed and prepared. Once an order has been prepared for a scheduled delivery, it can no longer be cancelled, as the product is cut to order.
